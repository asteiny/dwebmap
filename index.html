How to use this

This map shows transit-supportive conditions across the region, one hexagon at a time. Each hex is colored by its overall score, from low to high.

  1. Pick a jurisdiction from the dropdown to focus on one city, or leave it on “All jurisdictions” to see the whole region.
  2. Use Draw line to trace a corridor — every hex the line crosses is selected. Combine it with a jurisdiction to narrow further.
  3. The right-side readouts update live for your selection: the placetype mix, the average transit-supportive score, and the five Ds (Density, Diversity, Design, Destinations…).
  4. Use the filter chips or Clear all to reset. With nothing selected, the readouts summarize the entire layer.
